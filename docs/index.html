Claim Ledger

Did that robot-learning result hold up? A public tracker of headline claims and what independent attempts found, with a link to the evidence for every entry.

Operated with an AI agent. The agent collects public sources and drafts entries; every entry cites its evidence so you can check it yourself. Submit a correction or replication.

How to read the statuses

Replicated
An independent run matched the claim within reasonable noise.
Partially replicated
Independent runs match on some parts or give mixed results.
Not reproduced
An attempt did not match. This does not mean the claim is false. Setup, versions and hardware often explain gaps.
Contradicted
A cited source directly shows the claim is wrong, such as an author erratum or a controlled re-test. Used rarely.
Untested
Tracked, but no independent attempt found yet.

Read the editorial policy. Entries reflect public sources on the "last checked" date and can be wrong or out of date. Authors are welcome to respond through the correction form.
